Help & Service Guidelines

A simple guide to using the Taifa eCitizen demonstration portal.

①

Explore a service

Use the search box or choose a service category from the dashboard. You can browse without signing in.

②

Sign in or register

Create an account with a name and email address. Use fictional details only; this prototype does not verify identities.

③

Submit a demo request

Complete the request form and choose a county. A demo reference is generated for testing.

④

Track your request

Open My Requests to copy your reference. Tracking works only for requests in this browser and account.

🇰🇪

Kenyan service categories

Categories are inspired by common Kenyan public services, including civil registration, NTSA, business, land, education, labour and immigration.

🔒

Privacy & safety

Do not enter national ID numbers, passwords used elsewhere, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.
